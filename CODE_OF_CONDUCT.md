# Code of Conduct

Orbital Colony welcomes learners and contributors at every experience level. These expectations apply in project issues, pull requests, discussions, and spaces where someone represents the project.

- Treat people with respect. Focus feedback on the work and explain your reasoning.
- Give learners room to ask basic questions and make mistakes.
- Respect privacy and consent when sharing screenshots, code, or recordings.
- Keep project spaces free of harassment, discriminatory language, personal attacks, threats, and unwanted sexual attention.

## Reporting and enforcement

Contact the [project maintainer](https://github.com/kerolosonsy) when you experience or witness a violation. Ask for a private channel if your report involves sensitive details. Include enough information to identify the incident and any immediate concern.

Maintainers will review reports, limit disclosure to people needed to address them, and respond in proportion to the conduct. Actions can include removing content, issuing a warning, or restricting access to project spaces.
